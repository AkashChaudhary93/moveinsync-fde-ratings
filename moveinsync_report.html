1 · What this is, in plain words

MoveInSync asked: "Our Play Store ratings are lower than they should be. Work out why, tell us exactly what to fix first — specific enough that engineering starts tomorrow. And 'how are our ratings?' is a question we'll keep asking."

What I did (3 steps)

Step 1 — Built my own dataset. They gave no data, everything public. So I wrote a scraper (MoveInSync_scrape.py) that pulled the live listing + 2,000 newest reviews (400 per star) straight from Google Play. Saved as moveinsync_reviews_raw.csv. No guessing, no screenshots — real rows you can open in Excel.

Step 2 — Found the patterns. MoveInSync_analyze.py clustered every review into complaint buckets (booking bugs, GPS lies, OTP loops, crashes…) and counted hit-rates per star, then I hand-read 30+ full quotes to make sure the keywords weren't lying. Result: 6 failure factories, ranked.

Step 3 — Left something reusable. Because they'll keep asking "how are ratings?", I built rating_monitor.py — a 60-line script that re-checks the score every Monday, diffs it, and names the loudest complaint cluster. That's the FDE habit: every firefight leaves a tool behind.

3 · The 5 breaks in the employee journey

Break 1 — Self-booking creates the trip wrong (biggest 2★ driver)

"Even when I carefully select specific dates, the system creates bookings for completely different dates… reported as No Show… then asked to rate the trip." — June 2026

Auto-next-day toggle, missing monthly view, contradictory "trip not booked" push after success, silent 2-step shuttle change. One bug → strand → false No-Show → salary cut. Triple rage.

Break 2 — Pickup-time + live-track lies

"Google Maps always shows 3x the time shown in MoveInSync. It just acts as a booking tool." — Oct 2026

Frozen GPS dot, ETA off 3×, tracking locked until <60 min, employee-vs-driver sequence mismatch, gate dead-zones wipe driver card + OTP.

Break 3 — Boarding fails at the door

"Accidental touches resulting in trips unintentionally cancelled… 3 AM stress." — May 2026

OTP never arrives, forced logout loops, single-tap Cancel with zero confirm, wrong No-Show → salary deduction (highest rage per incident).

Break 4 — Foundation cracks

Splash-loop, crash on track, "reinstall to open" across v11–v12 (systemic, not one bad build), map-view lag, per-tap policy nag.

Break 5 — Help isn't there at 2 AM

"Cab at 2:30 AM, still nothing at 3:30, customer support not receiving the call." — Oct 2026

Helpline rings out, SOS cited non-functional, "Data can't be deleted" label fuels distrust.

4 · 30-day action plan (start Monday)

  • P0 · WEEK 1
    Booking truthfulness + No-Show amnesty. Kill auto-next-day default · booking receipt with server timestamp · monthly calendar back · idempotency key on book-tap · "vehicle crossed your pickup — confirm boarding" grace ping + one-tap dispute that pauses salary feed. Acceptance: wrong-date 1★ down 50% in 30 days.
  • P0 · WEEK 1
    Destructive-action guardrails (cheapest points). Cancel → mandatory 2-step confirm + 15-min undo; move it off the notification banner. Acceptance: zero one-tap cancels in telemetry.
  • P0 · WEEK 2
    Session + OTP survivability. 90-day device remember · parallel SMS+WhatsApp OTP + auto-read · offline trip card cached at booking (driver, vehicle, OTP readable with zero bars). Acceptance: login 1★ halved.
  • P1 · WEEK 3
    Honest tracking. Fuse ETA with traffic or show a window + "updated Xm ago" chip · unlock tracking early · single sequence source of truth. Acceptance: ETA ±3 min on 80% trips.
  • P1 · WEEK 3–4
    Notifications + cold-start. Dedupe contradictory push · priority channel for cab events · fix splash-loop + update migration + map lag; kill per-tap policy nag.
  • P2 · WEEK 4+
    Close the loop. In-app driver call/chat + callback queue · reply every 1–2★ in 48h · rating prompt ONLY after clean on-time trips (never after cancel/No-Show) · explain data-retention label in one sentence.

Honest math

Baseline ≈ 532,600 star-points. Converting just 20% of 7,338 1★ → 4★ = +0.04 (≈4.68); 40% = +0.08 (≈4.71). Real prize: 1★ share 6.4% → ~4–5%, which is what enterprise buyers screenshot — plus every prevented No-Show is a salary dispute avoided.
